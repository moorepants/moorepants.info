This is only his side of the story but the emails and voice recordings that he
posts gives a large amount of evidence for his position. He is brave to share
these videos, as most people that have such experiences do not speak out. I
can't say that I'm surprised by his allegations or description of his
experience. I'm not familiar with the professors he blames but TU Delft has
been under fire about "social safety" for some time now and with the `recent
confidential advisor report
<https://delta.tudelft.nl/en/article/structural-problems-with-social-safety-continue-to-persist-despite-end-of-mismanagement>`_
showing a continuing problem especially for PhD candidates. We've even `been
reprimanded again by the government very recently
<https://delta.tudelft.nl/en/article/fresh-warnings-for-tu-delft-over-its-approach-to-workload-and-inappropriate-behaviour>`_.
The stories I have heard at the coffee machine over the last 6 years here
unfortunately too often share alignment with Mr. Zhao's experience in various
ways.

If TU Delft has not given Mr. Zhao an independent review of his situation by
people that are not tied to the power structure that loomed over him, they
certainly should. Maybe the most striking thing in the videos is that it
doesn't seem his requests for an alternative path were ever granted. Regardless
of where the blame lies or whether justice should be served, this experience is
so egregious that it should be taken seriously by the university. I hope it has
and/or will be.
